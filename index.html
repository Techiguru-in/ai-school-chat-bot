How to Ask Better Questions

3 Golden Tips for Students:
  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Tell AI your class or grade level.
  • Ask AI to explain difficult topics with simple real-life examples.
Helpful phrasing to try:
  • Explain this in Hindi.
  • Explain this like I am in Class 6.
  • Give me an example.
